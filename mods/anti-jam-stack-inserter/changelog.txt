Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Anti-jam stack inserter: a stack inserter that swings without waiting for a full hand.
    - Startup setting "Item mode":
      - Smart swapping (default): built from and mined to stack inserter items; place it with an upgrade
        planner, blueprint, ghost or pipette. Switching a save to it turns existing anti-jam stack inserter
        items back into stack inserters.
      - Distinct item: its own item with the stack inserter's recipe.
      - Distinct item with quick swap: plus quick conversion recipes between stack and anti-jam stack inserters.
