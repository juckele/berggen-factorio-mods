Version: 0.2.0
  Changes:
    - Targets Factorio 2.1.
    - Killing the last demolisher of a territory turns its land into cleared land, and adding the mod to a save
      does the same for territories whose demolishers were already killed. Exploring cleared land doesn't spawn
      new demolishers.
    - Territories with a living demolisher retake neighbouring cleared land, one chunk at a time, explored or not.
    - Demolisher territory no longer expands into the starting area (setting, on by default). The starting area
      is recorded even with the setting off.
    - Expansion is global per surface and follows the map's enemy expansion settings: the cooldown uses the
      same evolution scaling as biter expansion (scaled to 1/20, since each expansion retakes one chunk), and
      turning enemy expansion off stops it. A tick override setting (10 for now, so testers can watch it)
      replaces the map cooldown when above 0.
    - Removed debug chat messages; debug logging is a setting.
    - Demolishers grow a size (small, medium, big) with odds based on their territory size, up to one size above
      what the map places at their distance.
    - Big demolishers with large territories travel to the centre of their territory, coil around it and lay a nest
      of 1 to 3 eggs, then return to their patrol. Hurting one while it nests stops it.
    - Eggs hatch into small demolishers with a one-chunk territory: soonest when their parent has died, then when
      there is cleared land next to their parent's territory, otherwise once old. Eggs can be destroyed.
    - Expansion, growth, nesting and hatching each follow the map's enemy expansion settings and evolution.
    - Warns in chat when enemy expansion is turned off in the map settings, which the mod needs.
    - Ping turret (after metallurgic science): very long range, very slow, and barely damaging; it lures
      demolishers to your defences. To ping only some sizes, add those demolishers to its priority targets and
      ignore other targets. Uses cheap ping shells.
    - In no-enemies mode the mod does nothing and says so in chat, instead of removing every (empty) territory.
    - New Vulcanus pollution layer, vibration: mining drills, pumps and offshore pumps make it, lava absorbs it
      quickly and volcanic rock steadily. It raises evolution on Vulcanus, and a demolisher investigates the loudest
      part of its territory once the vibration there gets high enough.
    - Fixed: killing a demolisher whose territory was entirely unexplored left its territory behind.
