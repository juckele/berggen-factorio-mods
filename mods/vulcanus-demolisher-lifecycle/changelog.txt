Version: 0.2.0
  Changes:
    - Targets Factorio 2.1.
    - Killing the last demolisher of a territory removes the territory, and adding the mod to a save removes
      territories whose demolishers were already killed. Removed land doesn't spawn new demolishers when it is
      explored later.
    - Demolisher territory no longer expands into the starting area (setting, on by default). The starting area
      is recorded even with the setting off.
    - Territory expansion runs on a configurable interval instead of every second.
    - Removed debug chat messages; debug logging is a setting.
